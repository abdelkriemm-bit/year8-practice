How it works

  • This is Quiz 3 (50 questions). Type your full name, then click Quiz 3 to start.
  • Every question is based on a text, like the real 0861 paper. Find the line number in the text (it is highlighted) and read it carefully.
  • Most reading questions need a written answer: a character trait with a quotation, ONE word, a phrase, or a three-word phrase copied exactly from the text. Spelling and word count matter!
  • As soon as you answer, your answer is marked and you see the model answer and why.
  • The target is 90% or more. Below 90%, study your weak topics and take the quiz again.
  • Send your result card to your teacher on Google Classroom.
